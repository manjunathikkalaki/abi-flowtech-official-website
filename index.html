Equipment Expertise

Machine Capabilities

We service, retrofit and manufacture components for all major CNC, hydraulic and automation machine types used in precision manufacturing across India.

🔩
CNC Machining

VMC – Vertical Machining Centre

The VMC is a CNC machine tool where the spindle axis is vertically oriented, ideal for drilling, boring, milling and tapping operations on flat surfaces. Flow Tech performs complete electrical rewiring, spindle replacement, ballscrew reconditioning and FANUC/Siemens control upgrades.

Spindle Service Ballscrew Recon FANUC / Siemens ATC Repair Axis Calibration
⚙️
CNC Machining

HMC – Horizontal Machining Centre

The HMC has a horizontally-oriented spindle, enabling multi-face machining in a single setup. Widely used in aerospace and automotive production. We handle pallet changers, rotary tables, ATC mechanisms, and full precision recalibration for all HMC brands.

Pallet System Rotary Table Aerospace Grade Full Overhaul Multi-axis
🔄
CNC Turning

CNC Turning Centre

CNC Turning Centres use a rotating workpiece and fixed cutting tool to produce cylindrical parts with high precision. Flow Tech services headstocks, turrets, tailstocks, live tooling and hydraulic chucks — restoring dimensional accuracy and repeat performance.

Headstock Recon Turret Repair Hydraulic Chuck Live Tooling Tailstock
💧
Hydraulic Systems

Hydraulic Power Pack

A Hydraulic Power Pack is a self-contained unit combining a motor, pump, reservoir and control valves that generates hydraulic power for machines and presses. Flow Tech designs and manufactures custom units from 1 HP to 100 HP with full pressure and flow control systems.

1–100 HP Range Pressure Control Directional Valves Filtration Custom Design
🏗️
Hydraulic Components

Hydraulic Cylinder & Press

Hydraulic cylinders convert fluid pressure into linear mechanical force, used across clamping, pressing, lifting and forming operations. Flow Tech manufactures single and double-acting cylinders, and hydraulic presses up to 500 tonnes with precision sealing and hard chrome finishing.

Single / Double Acting Up to 500T Chrome Finish Custom Bore Seal Repair
🤖
Automation / SPM

Special Purpose Machine (SPM)

SPMs are custom-built machines designed to perform a specific set of operations — drilling, tapping, reaming, milling or assembly. Flow Tech engineers SPMs to your exact cycle time, quality and throughput requirements with full PLC and pneumatic integration.

Drilling / Tapping Reaming / Milling PLC Control Pneumatics Jig & Fixture
